## Early ZPP Historical Archive

The [Early Zero-Point Protocol Historical Archive](./zpp-historical-archive/) preserves the earlier ZPP architecture and research lineage: routing prototypes, mesh/consensus experiments, development reports, manifests, telemetry, and the first-year longitudinal synthesis. These are historical records, **not** components of the later six-node runtime or evidence that the uncompleted Engine Study was performed. The active launcher in the later framework snapshot is `boot_mesh.sh`; historical references to `start_mesh.py` are retained for provenance.
