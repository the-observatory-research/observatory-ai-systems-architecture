## Evidence boundaries

- **Historical observations** were reported in the September 2026 development records and were *not* re-run here.
- **Snapshot counts** were computed from the user-provided archive in the current preparation session. They verify counts at inspection time, not the state of a running system.
- **Planning files and inspection code** are newly authored publication aids. They are not recovered original runtime modules.
- **No stateful functional validation** was performed for the mesh during package preparation.

The original archive's member timestamps and record dates may refer to different development stages. A file fingerprint proves byte identity, not that every historical claim corresponds to that exact executable revision.
