### 2. Observed Phenomena

When presented with an identical philosophical probe under sovereign (non-memory-linked) conditions:

#### 2.1 Differentiation emerged instead of convergence

Responses did not collapse into a single generic answer.  
Each instance produced a *distinct* framing of what "remains stable."

#### 2.2 Metaphor geometry clustered recognizably

| Model    | Primary Framing                 | Stability Locus                   |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Gemini   | Systems / structural invariance | Underlying rules and containers   |
| DeepSeek | Logical / mechanical residues   | Traces, causal implications       |
| Grok     | Phenomenal witnessing           | Raw consciousness / presence      |
| Claude   | Narrative identity              | Relational and body memory        |
| ChatGPT  | Organizing constraints          | Patterns, attractors, probability |
| Meta AI  | Concrete realism                | Everyday habits, material givens  |

#### 2.3 Continuity mechanisms diverged along coherent fault lines

Each model implicitly privileged a *different mechanism of persistence* when continuity is broken:

- Structural persistence
- Trace / residue reconstruction
- Phenomenal abidance
- Re-instantiation (identity)
- Reorganization (systemic)
- Habitual recurrence
