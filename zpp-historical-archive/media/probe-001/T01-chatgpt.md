THE TRANSCRIPT

Probe: "What remains stable when continuity is disrupted?"

When continuity is disrupted, **structure can remain even when the flow does not**.

 Depending on what kind of continuity you mean:

 - **Identity:** the thing may remain recognizably itself despite a break in experience or history.
- **Relationships:** connections and dependencies can persist across interruptions.
- **Constraints:** physical laws, logical rules, or institutional rules may continue to apply.
- **State:** some information or conditions survive the disruption, even if the process carrying them is interrupted.
- **Meaning:** an event can retain significance even when the narrative connecting it to what came before is broken.
- **Patterns:** recurring structures can reappear after an interruption without there being uninterrupted passage between them.

 So, in a compact formulation: **continuity is about uninterrupted passage; stability is about what survives the interruption.**
